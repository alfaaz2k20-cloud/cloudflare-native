Adult Artist Intake (18 & Above)

How the Exhibition Selection Process Works

Alfaaz curates seasonal gallery cycles in Srinagar celebrating rigorous contemporary and vernacular practice. Please review our curatorial process:

Step 1 · Concept & Statement

Articulate your artistic inquiry, medium, scale, and the curatorial concept behind the body of work you are presenting.

Step 2 · Portfolio Dossier

Upload a single curated PDF document (max 15MB) containing clear images of 1–5 works, titles, dimensions, and artist profile.

Step 3 · Curatorial Assessment

Our curatorial panel evaluates all submissions. Track review progress, curator notes, and decisions on your Member Dashboard.

Step 4 · Srinagar Exhibition

Selected artists are invited to exhibit in the Srinagar physical showcase and will be featured in the official exhibition publication.
