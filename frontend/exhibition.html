Ethos & Expectations

What to Expect When Participating

Alfaaz brings together artists, students, and cultural practitioners from diverse backgrounds across Jammu and Kashmir. Our guidelines ensure a dignified, supportive, and accessible experience for all.

01

Welcoming All Artistic Disciplines

We celebrate visual paintings, drawings, sketchbook studies, calligraphy, photography, sculpture, digital art, and poetic texts. No formal degree, art school background, or prior gallery exhibition history is required.

02

Honest Practice & Original Creations

Bring your genuine personal voice. We value practice sketches, experimental studies, and honest works-in-progress just as much as finished exhibition pieces. All submitted artwork must be your own original creation.

03

Curatorial Review Conducted with Care

Our curatorial circle reviews each entry with respect and empathy. While gallery physical space is limited, every applicant receives a fair assessment and can track their application status directly on their Member Dashboard.

04

Inclusive & Encouraging Space

We are committed to accessibility for participants of all linguistic backgrounds, neurodivergent individuals, young creators, and people with disabilities. If you require any accommodations, please let us know.

05

Privacy, Safety & Sovereign Data

Your contact details and uploaded artwork files are stored securely in our private sovereign vault and will never be shared publicly without your explicit written consent. For youth participants under 18, parental or guardian consent is respectfully gathered to ensure full safety.
