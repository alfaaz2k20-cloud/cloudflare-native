Guidelines & Process

How to Submit Your Art

Welcome to the Alfaaz Exhibition Open Call. Please read these simple steps before filling out your application below:

1
Choose Your Show

We organize separate shows for Below 18 (Children & Youth) and 18 & Above (Adults) so every artist is reviewed fairly in their peer group.

2
Share Your Details

Enter your name, town/city, and WhatsApp number so we can reach you with exhibition updates and next steps.

3
Upload Your Artwork (PDF)

Youth upload photos of drawings, paintings, or crafts in a PDF. Adults upload a recent works portfolio (max 15MB, PDF only).

4
Review & Results

Our curators review every piece with care and fairness. Your results will appear directly on your Alfaaz dashboard.
