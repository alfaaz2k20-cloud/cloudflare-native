How It Works // آسان طریقہ

Welcome to the Art Exhibition

We are so excited to see your artwork! Here is what happens when you send your work to us:

1. Pick an Exhibition

Choose the show for your age — we have separate exhibitions for Below 18 (Children & Youth) and 18 & Above (Adults).

2. Upload Your Art (PDF)

Fill in your details and upload a PDF of your drawings, paintings, or crafts.

3. Looked at with Care

Our team looks at every piece of art fairly and with lots of love and respect.

4. Saved in Our Collection

Your art is safely kept in our collection. You can always apply for future shows!
