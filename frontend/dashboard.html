How Your Dashboard Works

Welcome to your personal account on Alfaaz Collective. Here is a simple explanation of what each section does:

1. Display Name & Profile See your registered email and membership status. Click [Edit Name] anytime to change how your name appears on your passes and certificates.
2. Creative Clubs Apply to join specialized creative groups (Literature, Art & Craft, Cinema, Photography, or Philosophy) to collaborate with other artists.
3. Art Exhibition Track your exhibition artwork submission through 4 clear steps: Portfolio Submission, Review, Payment Proof, and Confirmed Selection.
4. Upcoming Events & My Passes Discover poetry readings, cultural gatherings, and workshops. Reserve your spot with WhatsApp, and your entry pass will appear under My Event Passes.
5. Ask AI Assistant Have questions about guidelines, submission criteria, or Kashmiri poetry? Ask our AI assistant anytime at the bottom of the page.
