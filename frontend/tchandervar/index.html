Bespoke Framing & Woodcraft

Our customized frames are designed to enhance—never overpower—the delicate rhythm of art. Drawing inspiration from the harmony found in Kashmiri pinjrakari woodwork, architecture, and natural grain, each frame brings quiet reverence to the artwork it surrounds.

About tchandervar

tchandervar connects local artists with individuals and institutions seeking to enhance their spaces with unique, high-quality artwork. We curate a diverse collection of paintings, sketches, and installations, carefully chosen to harmonise with diverse architectural environments. Clients can explore this edition or collaborate with us for bespoke site-specific curation—spanning residences, heritage hotels, offices, and cultural spaces.
