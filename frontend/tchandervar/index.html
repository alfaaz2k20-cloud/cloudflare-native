About tchandervar

tchandervar connects local artists with individuals who want to enhance their spaces with unique, high-quality artwork. We curate a diverse collection of paintings and other art forms, carefully chosen to fit a variety of environments. Clients can explore this collection or collaborate with us to find pieces that best suit their space, whether it's a residence, a hotel, office, or restaurant. Our service goes beyond just selling art—we provide personalized recommendations to create the perfect ambiance for each setting.
